Google OAuth & Data Use

Google/YouTube connection is optional. KLIGHTTEN MEDIA can be used for local media without connecting a Google account.

Current scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly

What the permission is used for

If you choose to connect Google/YouTube, KLIGHTTEN MEDIA uses the read-only scope for visible connected-account features. These can include showing the connected account/channel and loading playlists, subscriptions and liked videos into the app.

What the app does not do with this scope

  • It does not upload videos.
  • It does not delete YouTube content.
  • It does not post comments.
  • It does not rate or edit YouTube content.
  • It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

OAuth flow

  1. You initiate account connection from KLIGHTTEN MEDIA.
  2. The authorization flow opens through the browser/Google authorization interface.
  3. Google shows the requested permission before you approve it.
  4. After approval, KLIGHTTEN stores the authorization token locally on your computer so the account can remain connected.
  5. You can disconnect the account from KLIGHTTEN and revoke the app from your Google Account permissions.

Multiple accounts

KLIGHTTEN MEDIA can keep multiple connected YouTube accounts and lets you switch between them. Tokens are stored separately on the local device.

Data retention

Connected-account tokens remain on the device until you disconnect the account, remove the relevant local application data, or revoke the authorization. KLIGHTTEN MEDIA does not operate a cloud account database for these tokens.

Questions

See the Privacy Policy for the broader data-handling description and the Contact page for the current project support channel.
